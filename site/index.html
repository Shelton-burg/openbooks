01 — What it does

Everything a small business needs.

🧾

Invoices, quotes, bills, POs

Post a document and it lands in the ledger. Drafts stay drafts until you review them. Corrections are reversals, never silent edits.

🏦

Bank matching, line by line

Import your CSV and allocate every line. Transfers between your own accounts never touch profit or GST.

📊

GST & BAS the Australian way

Cash or accrual basis, purchase-evidence review, accountant packs with trial balance, P&L and contractor worksheet. The app never claims on your behalf.

🔒

Your data stays yours

Local database, verified backups, per-business restore. No cloud account, no telemetry, no lock-in. MIT licence — fork it, read it, keep it forever.

Three ways to run OpenBooks

  1. 1 Windows installer — download from the releases page, run it, and open OpenBooks from your Start menu. No Node, Docker or accounts needed. Your books live on your PC and are backed up daily.
  2. 2 Node 24 — npm ci --ignore-scripts · cp .env.example .env · npm run setup · npm run build · npm start, then open http://localhost:3000.
  3. 3 Docker — docker compose up -d. Full instructions in the README.

02 — The honest part

What OpenBooks is not — yet.

We'd rather tell you now than after your BAS is due. A working beta, built in the open, tested hard — still growing.

  • No payroll or Single Touch Payroll — yet
  • No live bank feeds — CSV import instead
  • No tax lodgment to the ATO
  • No card-payment integration
  • Windows installer unsigned (beta) — expected SmartScreen warning
  • Not accountant-certified — keep your current system during testing
